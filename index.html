Echo – Privacy Policy

Chauhan Studios · Effective 10 November 2026

This policy covers the mobile game Echo (Android package com.chauhanstudios.echo), published by Chauhan Studios.

Summary

Echo does not collect, store or share any personal data.

What the game does

  • The game works fully offline. It does not use the internet permission.
  • It has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics.
  • It asks for no device permissions (no camera, contacts, location, microphone or storage access).
  • It contains no third-party advertising or tracking software.

Data saved on your device

Your game progress (stars earned on each level) and your sound and music settings are saved only on your own device. This data never leaves the device. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset progress in the game, or by uninstalling the game.

Children

Echo is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds features that handle data (for example advertising or purchases), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
